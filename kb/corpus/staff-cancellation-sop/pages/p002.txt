                                                                                                        STRIPE & ACCOUNT STEPS



C O M P L E T E T H E C A N C E L L AT I O N


Follow the right cancellation path.
Use the member’s account to make the change whenever possible. This keeps Stripe,
GrantFind, and the confirmation email in sync.

  3       Paid Premium: step by step

             Open the member’s GrantFind profile
      1
             Have the member sign in, open Profile, and choose Manage Subscription. If you are assisting them, use the
             approved support/admin process—never ask for their password.

                GrantFind: Profile → Manage Subscription → Cancel My Subscription


             Confirm the warning
      2
             Make sure the member understands that paid cancellation removes Pro features and AI tools immediately. Ask them to
             confirm before proceeding.

             Verify the result
      3
             Wait for the success message. Re-open the profile if needed and confirm the account no longer shows an active
             Premium subscription.

             Confirm Stripe status if assisting manually
      4
             In Stripe, search the exact customer email and confirm the subscription is cancelled. Check that there is no future
             renewal scheduled.

                Stripe: Customers → search email → open customer → Subscriptions → verify Cancelled




  4       Premium Trial: step by step

             Open Profile → Manage Subscription
      1
             Confirm the account shows a Premium trial and note the displayed trial end date.

             Select Cancel My Trial
      2
             Confirm the member understands that the saved card will not be charged, while Premium access continues through the
             trial end date.

             Verify Stripe shows cancellation at period end
      3
             The subscription should remain trialing until the trial end, with cancellation scheduled and no conversion charge
             expected.

                Stripe: Customers → search email → open customer → Subscriptions → verify trial end and cancellation at period end




   Member-facing wording: “Your cancellation is confirmed. Your Premium access will remain available until [date]. Your saved card
   will not be charged for the trial.”
