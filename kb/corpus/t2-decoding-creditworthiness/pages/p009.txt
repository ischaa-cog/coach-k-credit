Impact of Ratings: Financial Outcomes
The assigned credit rating directly translates into tangible financial consequences, shaping an entity's access to capital and overall cost of borrowing.


                                                                                                                       Higher Ratings: Lower Risk, Better Terms
                                                                                                                       •     Significantly lower perceived default risk.
                                                                                                                       •     Access to more attractive interest rates on loans and bonds.
                                                                                                                       •     Increased investor confidence and broader market access.
                                                                                                                       •     Enhanced ability to secure financing for growth and expansion.
