The Indian Landscape: Key Rating Agencies
India's financial ecosystem is supported by several prominent credit rating agencies that cater to diverse segments, from individual consumers to large corporations and government bodies.


                                                                                                                   CIBIL (TransUnion CIBIL)
                                                                                                                   Primarily focuses on individual credit reports and scores, playing a pivotal role in consumer lending decisions.
                                                                                                                   They also provide comprehensive company credit reports.



                                                                                                                   ICRA (An Moody's Affiliate)
                                                                                                                   A leading full-service credit rating agency in India, providing ratings for corporate entities, state governments,
                                                                                                                   and various financial instruments, alongside detailed analytical research.
