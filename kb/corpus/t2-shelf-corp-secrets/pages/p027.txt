The Cost of Reinstatement
It must be noted that a for profit’s corporation’s reinstatement fee is $150 per year plus $600. With the waiver, and a corporation that
is in INACT/UA status, this corporation will cost you $300. An LLC in INACT/UA status will cost you $271. Although Corporations tend to
get higher lines of credit, an aged LLC for $271 is well worth it.
