

I have highlighted a number of aspects of the result page:

   a- After doing a search you can move forward and backward among company names,
      so in doing research you will start with a chosen search name and simply go through
      the list.

   b- This is the “Status” of the corporation. ACT= active, INACT=inactive,
      INACT/UA=Inactive but unavailable. Strangely enough the corporations that we
      mainly want are the ones that say INACT/UA. The unavailable simply means that no
      one can currently incorporate another corporation with the same name because it
      has recently gone inactive. This is vital because in reinstating the corporations, we
      will be paying a certain fee per year of inactivity and these have the lowest inactive
      time. You can also research companies that are INACT but when you get experience,
      you will realize that you will come across many companies that have been inactive
      for more than 10 years. By only using the INACT/UA corps, this reduces the amount
      of research that you will have to do before you find a good corp. This does not mean
      that I do not research INACT corps as well, but I am just giving you the quickest
      method to success.

   c- These are the names of the corporations. You will have to click each link to view
      more details on the corporation.
