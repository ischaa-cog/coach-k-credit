THE SECRETARY OF STATE WEBSITE OF CHOICE

elections to business incorporation for the each state. In this ebook we have covered using the
FL secretary of state website in detail. It can be found at www.sunbiz.org. This was done
because in my opinion, this is a very simple website with many resources. Other SOS websites
