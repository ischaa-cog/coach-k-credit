

CHECKING YOUR EXPERIAN BUSINESS FILE
Once you have paid the initial vendors and waited long enough, you will like to check your
Experian business file. You will need to go to www.smartbusinesscreditreports.com and
purchase one of their products. I suggest that you purchase one of their Business Credit
Advantage Subscriptions plans as those are the most comprehensive files that they offer.




OVERCOMING POTENTIAL PROBLEMS
There are a number of potential problems that may arise when acquiring these shelf
corporations and building credit on them.

Some of them are:

   1. The credit files being wiped. This is something that has been spoken about everywhere
      in the business credit arena. In order to avoid this, you must ensure that when you call
      to change the corporate information, and DnB returns your call to confirm the
      information, when asked how long you have owned this corporation, calmly say
      something like “since 2003”. This will appear on your credit file.
   2. There may be an “Inactive” note on your credit file. DnB, being who they are put an
      inactive note on some of the files and some of the files, they seem to have missed it. If
      this is the case you do not have to worry. Many lenders seem to lover look it but you will
      not simply get denied for this. The very most you may have to do it fax in the certificate
      of good standing, as mentioned previously.
   3. Virtual office issues. I have noticed, when using those large virtual office names (never
      use ups), the vendors may ask for a business license. This is simply to confirm that you
      do business at that address. In most cases, corporations do not need a business license
      to operate in FL so a utility bill will be fine. I usually fax in an ATT bill.
