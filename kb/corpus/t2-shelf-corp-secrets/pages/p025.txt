

STEP 5
REINSTATE THE CORPORATION

The reinstatement process is very simple for corporations that have been inactive for less than
a year. i.e. Those that have a status of INACT/UA.

Once you are on the profile page of the corporation, you can reinstate it online. First, click
“Efiling Services”, then “Reinstatement Filing” as seen in the image below. Please not that if you are
not working with a corporation that is not in the “INACT/UA” status, you can call 850-245-5069 to complete the
process.

                                                                                                       Note
                                                                                                       When reinstating the
                                                                                                       corporation, it is
                                                                                                       important to pay the extra
                                                                                                       $5 or $8.25 for the
                                                                                                       certificate of status.
