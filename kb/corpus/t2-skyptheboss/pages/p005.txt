1099A Purchasing Process

DISCLAIMER:
THE FOLLOWING DOCUMENT IS THE 1099A-
ACQUISTION OR ABANDOMENT OF SECURED
PROPERTY. THIS IS THE DOCUMENT THAT IS MEANT
FOR THE BENEFICIARY TO FILL OUT. THIS PROCESS WILL
SHOW YOU HOW TO FILL IT OUT ONLINE AT
1099ONLINE.COM.


In order to understand this process, you must
understand that they’re two parties, The Beneficiary
and the Trustee. The Beneficiary can only benefit from
this process. The Trustee is the person that the
Beneficiary TRUST to act on their behalf to access the
trust for the Beneficiaries benefit. The Trustee CAN NOT
be the Beneficiary and the Beneficiary CAN NOT be the
Trustee. IF you try to act as the other party, Men In
Black Suits will pull up and have a conversation with
you, or worse.
