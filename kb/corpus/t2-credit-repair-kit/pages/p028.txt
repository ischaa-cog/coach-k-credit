MOVING FORWARD
                                Congratulations!  Now that you have read this
                                handbook, you should:
                                    Realize that you are not the only person who has
                                    credit problems .. and that you can resolve the
                                    situation, improve your credit status, and get the
                                    credit that you deserve.
                              Know that the first step in rebuilding your credit
                              rating is organizing all of your financial
                              information and reviewing your pattern of bill
                              payment. By doing this, you can see exactly how
   your behavior can impact your credit status.
   Understand that a positive mental outlook has a profound effect on your
   ability to restore your good credit rating.
   Be comfortable with the terminology of the credit industry.
   Determine how the available options, including refinancing and bankruptcy,
   can help – or hurt – your situation.
   Be familiar with the ways in which federal regulations regarding the behavior
   of credit reporting and debt collection agencies can protect you.
   Understand the workings of a credit bureau and how to request – and read –
   your own credit report.
   Be aware of how to correct errors in your credit reports
   Understand the techniques for re-establishing and keeping good credit.

With time, your preparation, persistence, and hard work
should pay off. But, remember – You are responsible for
maintaining your own good credit rating.

Good luck!
