FINE-TUNING YOUR MENTAL OUTLOOK

Other people get credit … Why can’t I?
One of the primary reasons that individuals experience
difficulty in trying to fix or restore their credit situations has
to do with the “mindset” that they carry around with them.

Many people feel completely overwhelmed, depressed,
and incapable of action. They look at a rejection from a
credit company as a given, something that they expected
to happen, and maybe even something that they deserved!

    “I had a feeling I would get turned down. This is awful. I’ll never get approved
    for a loan at this rate!”

But, by accepting this situation, you are taking the position of a victim, one who
‘deserves’ bad things to happen to him or her.

Clearly, in order for you to have a fighting chance of restoring your credit, you
must first work on your mental attitude towards your situation. You must clearly
define your goals and outline a clear, precise course of action to achieve those
goals.

It is critical to confront the self-defeating negative thoughts that have resulted in
immobility and paralysis! This can definitely improve your mood and strengthen your
ability to follow through on your plan to restore your credit now – not later, not in ten
years, but now!

How can I change my negative outlook to a positive one?
Here are some examples of how you can view the challenge of turning around your
credit situation from a negative standpoint, and how you can view it from a rational,
positive one:


                Negative Thought: “I’m never going to get out of this. My finances are
                horrible, and no company will ever give me credit again!”



Rational Thought: “Even though I have been rejected in the past, that
doesn’t necessarily mean I will always be rejected by credit companies,
especially if I work at it.”
