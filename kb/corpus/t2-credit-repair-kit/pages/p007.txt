                        A GREAT IDEA!
As a working tool along the way towards credit repair, take the time
to monitor your thoughts on a weekly basis. Keep a journal of your
negative thoughts, and try to think of a more positive, rational way to
counter those thoughts. As you become more mobilized, you will
notice a trend: it gets easier to think of more positive things to tell
yourself, and you are becoming more assertive as a result.

Remember, this process takes time, but it can be literally a life-altering pursuit.
