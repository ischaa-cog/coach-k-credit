         There is two (2) types of business credit that we’re
talking about here: “short credit” and “long credit.” You can
easily and quickly build up a solid and stellar business credit
track record by simply using Experian Business. This means
that all you need to do is raise your personal credit FICO, set
up your corporation and EIN, and start applying for
unsecured business credit cards. Use those credit cards, pay
them off, and keep using them. This happens relatively
quickly (within a couple of months) but only provides you
with something called “short credit.” You are essentially
limited to about $100,000 or more collectively among many
unsecured business credit cards.
         But what if you need more money?
         You’ll have to dive in deeper and start actually
rolling up your sleeves to adequately and properly build up
your business credit. And yes, this WILL take some time.
         This is when you’re going to have to start setting up
your profile with DNB, to start building what we call “long
credit.”
