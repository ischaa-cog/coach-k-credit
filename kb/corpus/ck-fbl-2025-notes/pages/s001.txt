COACH K — SUMMARY
Coach K delivered a tactical session focused on helping entrepreneurs access 5-6 figures in
grant funding to grow and scale their businesses. She shared her journey from being
terminated by Wells Fargo for teaching funding strategies to now working in the grants
space. She provided attendees with a step-by-step game plan on how to find, apply for, and
win grant money regardless of business size, industry, or nonprofit status. The session
emphasized mindset, consistency, preparation, and community.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
1. Game plan is everything. Entrepreneurs must start with clarity and direction. Knowing
exactly why funding is needed and how it will be used is key to crafting successful
applications.
2. Think bigger than your industry. Don't just search for grants in your niche. Leverage
broader categories like "minority-owned," "startup," "community impact" to increase
opportunity.
3. Preparation wins. Create a "grant vault" with key business documents -- EIN, LLC
paperwork, pitch deck, headshot, budget plan -- to streamline the application process and
avoid missed deadlines.
4. Solve a real problem. Funders want to support ventures that solve clear, well-defined
problems. You must articulate how your business is the solution in your grant narrative.
5. THE REAL FRAMEWORK FOR APPLICATIONS:
   - Relatable: share a personal connection or story.
   - Educate: clearly explain the problem you solve.
   - Action: outline how the funds will be used.
   - Lovable: make your business irresistible to funders.
6. Consistency is key. The community goal is to apply for one grant a day. Repetition
builds skill and confidence, turning early small wins into bigger opportunities.
7. Position yourself for more. Combine for-profit and nonprofit strategies to unlock
six-figure grants. You can even collaborate with nonprofits to access restricted funds.
