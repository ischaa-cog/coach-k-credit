PRACTICAL ACTION STEPS
1. Write a check to your "why" with a date and dollar amount -- use it as a visual
commitment to your goal.
2. Create your grant vault: EIN, LLC docs, business email, address, business overview,
headshot, website, and pitch video.
3. Use AI to enhance your applications: ask it to review, rewrite, or optimize your grant
responses and align them with grant guidelines.
4. Film a pitch video (90-120 seconds) explaining who you are, your business, your needs,
and how you'll use the grant. A teleprompter app helps you stay concise.
5. Apply for at least one grant a day. Make it part of your business operations.
6. Explore partnerships: if you don't have a nonprofit, collaborate with one to increase
access to funding.
7. Start searching on GuideStar.org for foundations that have given large amounts recently,
and customize letters of intent to get ahead of the competition.
