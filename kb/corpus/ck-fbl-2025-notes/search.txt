Blake Nathan's 501(c)(3) material is genuinely useful and genuinely his.] COACH K — SUMMARY Coach K delivered a tactical session focused on helping entrepreneurs access 5-6 figures in grant funding to grow and scale their businesses. She shared her journey from being terminated by Wells Fargo for teaching funding strategies to now working in the grants space. She provided attendees with a step-by-step game plan on how to find, apply for, and win grant money regardless of business size, industry, or nonprofit status. The session emphasized mindset, consistency, preparation, and community. KEY TAKEAWAYS 1. Game plan is everything. Entrepreneurs must start with clarity and direction. Knowing exactly why funding is needed and how it will be used is key to crafting successful applications. 2. Think bigger than your industry. Don't just search for grants in your niche. Leverage broader categories like "minority-owned," "startup," "community impact" to increase opportunity. 3. Preparation wins. Create a "grant vault" with key business documents -- EIN, LLC paperwork, pitch deck, headshot, budget plan -- to streamline the application process and avoid missed deadlines. 4. Solve a real problem. Funders want to support ventures that solve clear, well-defined problems. You must articulate how your business is the solution in your grant narrative. 5. THE REAL FRAMEWORK FOR APPLICATIONS: - Relatable: share a personal connection or story. - Educate: clearly explain the problem you solve. - Action: outline how the funds will be used. - Lovable: make your business irresistible to funders. 6. Consistency is key. The community goal is to apply for one grant a day. Repetition builds skill and confidence, turning early small wins into bigger opportunities. 7. Position yourself for more. Combine for-profit and nonprofit strategies to unlock six-figure grants. You can even collaborate with nonprofits to access restricted funds. KEY QUOTES FROM COACH K - "It's not if you get the grant. It's when you get the grant -- if you execute." - "If you can't find the opportunity, create it. If you can't see the path, pave it." - "Be the missing piece in your industry. That's how you dominate funding." - "Poverty ends with me, and wealth begins with me." - "When someone gives you a blessing, figure out how you can bless someone else." PRACTICAL ACTION STEPS 1. Write a check to your "why" with a date and dollar amount -- use it as a visual commitment to your goal. 2. Create your grant vault: EIN, LLC docs, business email, address, business overview, headshot, website, and pitch video. 3. Use AI to enhance your applications: ask it to review, rewrite, or optimize your grant responses and align them with grant guidelines. 4. Film a pitch video (90-120 seconds) explaining who you are, your business, your needs, and how you'll use the grant. A teleprompter app helps you stay concise. 5. Apply for at least one grant a day. Make it part of your business operations. 6. Explore partnerships: if you don't have a nonprofit, collaborate with one to increase access to funding. 7. Start searching on GuideStar.org for foundations that have given large amounts recently, and customize letters of intent to get ahead of the competition.
