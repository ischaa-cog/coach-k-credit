GRANT MASTERY MASTERCLASS — WORKBOOK
www.GrantMasteryMasterclass.com (Coach K)

This workbook is designed to guide you through the session and help you apply the concepts
to your business.

1. WELCOME & CLASS OVERVIEW
Masterclass objectives:
- Learn how to secure grant funding for your business or organization.
- Understand the steps to structure your business for ultimate success.
- Discover where and how to find grant opportunities.

3. STEP 1: BUILDING THE FOUNDATION
Checklist:
- Establish a legal entity (LLC, nonprofit, etc.).
- Write a business plan.
- Develop an elevator pitch.
- Ensure strong online brand presence (business email, social media).

Elevator pitch template:
"I am [Your Name], and I help [Target Audience] achieve [Result or Solution] by [Your
Unique Method]."

Define your mission:
1. What problem does your business solve?
2. How does your business impact the community?

4. STEP 2: FINDING GRANT OPPORTUNITIES
Types of grants:
1. Government Grants — local, state, and federal opportunities.
2. Foundation Grants — nonprofits and charitable foundations.
3. Corporate Grants — offered by companies like Chick-fil-A or AT&T.

5. STEP 3: QUALIFYING & APPLYING FOR GRANTS
Action plan template:
1. Problem: what is the key issue in your community/industry?
2. Solution: how does your business address this issue?
3. Impact: how will grant funding help you solve this problem?

Practice application notes: Grant Name / Grant Requirements / How Your Business Qualifies.

6. TIPS FOR SUCCESS
1. Success loves speed — take action immediately.
2. Be Relatable — share authentic stories about your business.
3. Be Ready — always have a business plan and key documents ready.

7. REFLECTION & COMMITMENT
1. What is the first step you will take after this class?
2. How will you hold yourself accountable?
Commitment: "I commit to applying for at least ___ grants within the next 30 days."
