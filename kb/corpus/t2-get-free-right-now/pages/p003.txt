                            INTERNATIONAL BILL OF EXCHANGE
                                  HIGHEST LEVEL INSTRUMENT
WHAT WERE DOING IS INTERNATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL BILL OF EXCHANGE OPERATES UNDER
THE UPU TREATY WHICH IS THE POSTAL TREATY.

   •   CREATE YOUR OWN INTERNATIONAL BILL OF EXCHANGE TO PAY YOUR BILLS WITH.

THE BILL OF EXCHANGE IS A KIND OF PAPER IN ORDER THAT IT’S HOLDER SHALL ENTITLE THE DEBTOR
NAMED IN THE DOCUMENT TO PAY A CERTAIN NUMBER OF PAYMENTS. THE BILL OF EXCHANGE
REPRESENTS A MEANS OF PAYMENT AND AN INSTRUMENT OF PROVIDING THE PAYMENT. IT IS AN
UNCONDITIONAL ORDER GIVEN BY THE DRAWER TO THE DRAWEE TO PAY A CERTAIN AMOUNT TO THE
PAYEE LISTED ON THE BILL OF EXCHANGE. THE BILL OF EXCHANGE IS GENERALLY A PAPER ON THE
ORDERS BUT IT CAN BE ISSUED AS VALUE PAPER TO A NAME. IT IS SUCH PROMISSORY NOTES WHERE
THE ISSUER BECOMES A CLAUSE NOT BY ORDER. THE BILL OF EXCHANGE THOUGH IT APPEARS AS A
MEANS OF PAYMENT AND AN INSTRUMENT FOR SECURING THE PAYMENT IT ALSO APPEARS AS A
MEANS OF INTERNATIONAL PAYMENT, BECAUSE THE BILL OF EXCHANGE CAN BECOME A MODERN
INSTRUMENT OF CREDITING AND PAYMENT. SO, THE BILL OF EXCHANGE HAS A DAUL ROLE, IT’S AN
INSTRUMENT AND PAYMENT.

BECAUSE THE BANKS ARE USING A CREDIT TRANSFER SYSTEM WHEN IT COMES TO OUR SECURITIES,
THIS IS WHY THE INTERNATIONAL BILL OF EXCHANGE IS THE HIGHEST LEVEL OF AN EXCHANGE.
EXAMPLE: YOU WENT TO THE CAR LOTS AND GOT A PURCHASE ORDER AND FOUND A TRUSTEE, YOU
SIGN THE PURCHASE ORDER LIKE A CHECK AND YOU SENT THEM A BILL OF EXCHANGE WITH IT, I THINK
YALL KNOW THE REST.

YOU CAN THINK OF A BILL OF EXCHANGE AND CREATE IT AND TAKE IT TO YOUR BANK AND TELL THEM
TO DEPOSIT IT AND THEY WILL TAKE IT. ALWAYS PUT YOUR STAMPS ON ANY OF YOUR CONTRACTS.
YOU COULD EVEN ADD AN ADDITIONAL INTERNATIONAL BILL OF EXCHANGE.

NOTE: WHEN YOU’RE DOING THE 1099 C PROCESS, IN YOUR INSTRUCTIONS LETTER YOU MAKE SURE
YOU REQUEST THAT THEY GIVE YOU BACK YOUR TENDER. MAKE SURE YOU USE THE WORD TENDER
BECAUSE YOU HAVE SENT THEM THE WRONG TENDER. YOU SENT THEM AN IOU AND BY LAW YOU
HAVE ACTUALLY NOT PAID YOUR BILL. WHETHER IT BE AN INTERNATIONAL BILL OF EXCHANGE OR
INTERNATIONAL PROMISSORY NOTE, YOU CAN MAKE IT NEGOTIABLE OR NON-NEGOTIABLE AND IF ITS
