THE BILL OF EXCHANGE IS A KIND OF PAPER IN ORDER THAT IT’S HOLDER SHALL ENTITLE THE DEBTOR
NAMED IN THE DOCUMENT TO PAY A CERTAIN NUMBER OF PAYMENTS. THE BILL OF EXCHANGE
REPRESENTS A MEANS OF PAYMENT AND AN INSTRUMENT OF PROVIDING THE PAYMENT. IT IS AN
UNCONDITIONAL ORDER GIVEN BY THE DRAWER TO THE DRAWEE TO PAY A CERTAIN AMOUNT TO THE
PAYEE LISTED ON THE BILL OF EXCHANGE. THE BILL OF EXCHANGE IS GENERALLY A PAPER ON THE
ORDERS BUT IT CAN BE ISSUED AS VALUE PAPER TO A NAME. IT IS SUCH PROMISSORY NOTES WHERE
THE ISSUER BECOMES A CLAUSE NOT BY ORDER. THE BILL OF EXCHANGE THOUGH IT APPEARS AS A
MEANS OF PAYMENT AND AN INSTRUMENT FOR SECURING THE PAYMENT IT ALSO APPEARS AS A
MEANS OF INTERNATIONAL PAYMENT, BECAUSE THE BILL OF EXCHANGE CAN BECOME A MODERN
INSTRUMENT OF CREDITING AND PAYMENT. SO, THE BILL OF EXCHANGE HAS A DAUL ROLE, IT’S AN
INSTRUMENT AND PAYMENT.

BECAUSE THE BANKS ARE USING A CREDIT TRANSFER SYSTEM WHEN IT COMES TO OUR SECURITIES,
THIS IS WHY THE INTERNATIONAL BILL OF EXCHANGE IS THE HIGHEST LEVEL OF AN EXCHANGE.
