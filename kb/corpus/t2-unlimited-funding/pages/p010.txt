Active vs. Passive Income

Active income is the one that you EARN – that you work for, regardless if you work
for yourself or someone else.

Active income comes in only when you work. As soon as you stop, the income stops.

Do you know that most Real Estate investors are earning active income and most
stay broke?

They buy, fix and sell properties. Or they flip properties. That is active income.

If you’re not sure if that’s active income, just ask yourself… will their income stop if
they stop buying and fixing houses? The answer is yes – their income will stop!
