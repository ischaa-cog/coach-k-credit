GRANT RESOURCES

GRANTS.GOV: the official website of the U.S. government for finding federal grants. Provides
a comprehensive database of grant opportunities from various federal agencies.
FOUNDATION CENTER (now CANDID): a leading source of information on philanthropy, nonprofits,
and grants. Provides a comprehensive database of grants from foundations, corporations, and
other funding sources.
GRANTWATCH.COM: offers a wide range of grants including government, foundation, and corporate
grants. Allows you to search by category, location, and keywords.
GRANTSTATION: a subscription-based service providing access to a database of grant
opportunities including government grants, foundation grants, and corporate giving programs.
Also offers training resources and grant-writing tools.
THE CHRONICLE OF PHILANTHROPY: focuses on news and trends in the philanthropic sector. Often
features grant opportunities and provides insights into the world of grants and fundraising.
CORPORATE GIVING DIRECTORY: many corporations have their own grant programs and corporate
giving initiatives. Provides information on corporate grants and CSR programs.
STATE AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT WEBSITES: often offer grants and funding opportunities for
economic development, arts and culture, education, and community development.
PROFESSIONAL ASSOCIATIONS AND NONPROFIT NETWORKS: explore associations and networks related
to your field of interest. These often have grant programs or can provide information on
finding grants specific to your industry.

Remember to carefully review the eligibility criteria and application requirements for each
grant opportunity to ensure they align with your organization's goals and objectives.
