CHAPTER 2: PREPARING TO WRITE A GRANT PROPOSAL

2.1 IDENTIFYING YOUR BUSINESS GOALS AND OBJECTIVES
Mission and Vision: clearly articulate your business's mission and vision statements. These
provide a foundation for your grant proposal and demonstrate your purpose and long-term
aspirations.
Specific Objectives: break down your overall goals into specific, measurable objectives.
These should be realistic and aligned with the scope of the grant you are pursuing. Clearly
define the outcomes you aim to achieve through the proposed project.
Impact and Benefit: consider the broader impact your project will have on your business,
community, or target audience. Highlight how the grant funding will contribute to positive
change and address existing challenges.

2.2 DEVELOPING A COMPELLING PROJECT IDEA
Identify a Need: determine a specific problem or need that your project will address.
Conduct market research, analyze industry trends, and gather data to support the existence
of this need.
Innovative Approach: showcase how your project idea offers a unique and innovative solution.
Highlight any novel strategies, technologies, or methodologies that set your project apart.
Feasibility and Sustainability: ensure your project idea is feasible and sustainable in the
long term. Consider resources, expertise, partnerships, and potential challenges. Grantors
want to invest in projects with a high likelihood of success and long-term impact.

2.3 ASSESSING YOUR ELIGIBILITY AND FIT
Eligibility Criteria: review the grant guidelines and eligibility requirements thoroughly.
Pay attention to geographical limitations, industry focus, organizational structure, and
project size. Proceed only if you meet the specified criteria.
Alignment with Grantor's Priorities: evaluate how well your project aligns with the
grantor's mission, goals, and funding priorities. Research the grantor's previous grant
awards to gain insights into their preferences and areas of interest.
Fit with Your Business Strategy: assess how the grant aligns with your overall business
strategy. Consider if the funding will complement your existing operations and contribute
to long-term growth and sustainability.

2.4 BUILDING RELATIONSHIPS WITH GRANTORS
Attend Networking Events: participate in workshops, conferences, and seminars where grantors
are present. Engage in conversations, ask questions, and exchange contact information.
Utilize Online Platforms: join online communities, forums, and social media groups related
to grants and funding. Participate in discussions, share insights, and connect with grantors
or other entrepreneurs in your industry.
