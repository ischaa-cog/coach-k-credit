SAMPLE GRANT PROPOSAL TEMPLATE
Use this template to write your grants.

[Your Name] / [Your Organization] / [Address] / [City, State, ZIP] / [Email] / [Phone] / [Date]
[Grantor's Name] / [Grantor's Organization] / [Address] / [City, State, ZIP]
Subject: Grant Proposal for [Project Title]
Dear [Grantor's Name],
I am writing to submit a grant proposal on behalf of [Your Organization] for funding support
for our project titled [Project Title].

1. Executive Summary: [Provide a brief overview of the project, including its objectives, the
target population it aims to serve, and the anticipated outcomes. Highlight the unique
aspects of the project and its potential impact.]
2. Introduction and Background: [Provide background on your organization -- mission, history,
previous accomplishments. Explain the need or problem the project addresses, supported by
relevant data and statistics. Describe how the project aligns with your mission and values.]
3. Project Goals and Objectives: [Clearly articulate the specific goals and objectives.
Ensure they are measurable, time-bound, and aligned with your mission and community needs.]
4. Project Activities and Methodology: [Describe the activities and strategies implemented to
achieve the goals. Explain the rationale behind each activity and how they address the
identified need. Outline the project timeline, including start and end dates for each.]
5. Target Population and Beneficiaries: [Identify the specific demographic or community group
that will benefit. Describe their characteristics, needs, and how the project will directly
impact their lives.]
6. Evaluation Plan: [Discuss the evaluation methods and metrics used to assess effectiveness
and measure achievement of objectives. Explain how data will be collected, analyzed, reported.]
7. Budget: [Provide a detailed budget including all anticipated expenses and their
justifications. Break down costs by category -- personnel, supplies, equipment, and other
relevant expenditures. If applicable, include in-kind contributions or other funding sources.]
8. Sustainability Plan: [Outline your plan to sustain the project beyond the grant period.
Discuss how you will secure additional funding, engage community partners, or leverage
resources to ensure long-term success and continuation.]
9. Organizational Capacity: [Highlight your capacity and experience implementing similar
projects. Provide information about key personnel, their qualifications, and any relevant
partnerships or collaborations.]
10. Conclusion: [Summarize the key points, emphasizing the project's potential impact and
alignment with the grantor's funding priorities. Express gratitude for the opportunity and
your willingness to provide additional information.]

Sincerely, [Your Name] / [Your Title] / [Your Organization]
