  Coach K Insight on Identity Theft

  “I’ve seen clients lose years of credit-building work in weeks
  because someone got their Social Security number and
  opened multiple accounts before they noticed.

  The best protection is prevention:
     Freeze your credit
     Monitor daily
     Never give your SSN unless you initiated the request

  A credit freeze costs nothing—and blocks everything. Do it
  today.”

Identity theft is not rare—and it’s not random. The difference between
temporary damage and long-term devastation comes down to speed
and action.

   Detect early
   Act immediately
   Document everything
   Protect yourself moving forward

Your credit is an asset. Protect it like one.
