      WHAT IS CREDIT REALLY?

      Credit is the financial world's trust system. When a lender,
      landlord, or company extends credit to you, they are betting
      that you will pay them back. Your credit history is the record of
      every financial commitment you've ever made — and whether
      you kept it. Your credit SCORE is the shorthand number they
      use to decide whether to bet on you, and at what price.

      Your credit score is not just a number. It is the
      price you pay for money.

                Higher score = cheaper money
                Cheaper money = more access
                More access = more freedom
