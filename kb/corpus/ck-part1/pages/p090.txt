The Better Business Bureau (BBB) — Verify
Before You Trust
The Better Business Bureau is a nonprofit organization that tracks:
    Business ratings
    Complaint history
    Accreditation status

Before you do business with ANY credit repair company, debt
settlement firm, grant writing service, financial advisor, or lender —
👉 Always check them with the BBB first.

Warning Signs of Credit Repair Scams

 The credit repair industry attracts scams. Protect yourself by
 watching for these red flags:
      Promises to remove accurate negative information (illegal)
      Large upfront fees before any work is done (violates CROA)
      Advice to dispute everything regardless of accuracy
      Suggestions to create a new identity (EIN/CPN fraud)
      No written contract outlining services
      No BBB presence or verifiable reviews
      Guaranteed score increases within a timeframe
 👉 If you see these signs—walk away.
