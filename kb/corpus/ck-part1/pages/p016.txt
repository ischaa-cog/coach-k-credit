Why This Changes Everything

This is why I always tell people: don’t let your personal credit
situation delay your business credit journey.

I’ve seen clients with credit scores in the 500s — even people
with bankruptcies — get approved for real vendor accounts like
Uline, Quill, Grainger, and other Tier 1 suppliers once their
business was properly structured.

In some cases, they were approved for $10,000–$25,000 in net-
30 credit within 60 days, not because their personal credit was
strong — but because their business looked legitimate.
That’s the shift.


The Setup Is Everything

If you want grantors to take you seriously, your business has to
look complete and consistent:

   Business name, address, phone, and EIN must match
   everywhere exactly

   Your business phone must be listed in 411 directories

   You need a DUNS number to establish your business credit
   identity

   Your business should have a basic website and professional
   email

   Your first orders should be realistic — usually $50 to $150 is
   enough to start

   Once you’re approved, how you pay matters more than
   anything — early payments build stronger vendor trust and
   better PAYDEX scores
