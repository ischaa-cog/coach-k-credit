Goodwill Letter Formula

Your letter must include all of these elements — in order:


Brief, honest story:
        What caused the hardship (job loss, medical, family
        emergency)
Take ownership:
        No excuses, no blame — just accountability
Show current positive behavior:
        How long since the incident you’ve paid on time
Specific, direct ask:
        “I am requesting removal of the late payment from
        [Month/Year]”
Express genuine gratitude:
        Be human, not transactional
Keep it to one page:
        Executives don’t read essays
Send certified mail:
        Creates accountability and paper trail
Follow up in 30 days:
        If no response
