Pre-Qualification vs. Pre-Approval — Critical Distinction

Before applying for ANY credit, always look for Pre-
Qualification first.

Pre-Qualification
✔ Uses Soft Pull
✔ No score impact
✔ Shows approval odds
Pre-approval is a step further and often involves a hard pull.
If a lender says 'check if you pre-qualify,' use it. If they take
you straight to a full application, that is a hard pull. Ask first.



  Coach K Strategy
  When building business credit, always ask: "Is your
  approval process a hard pull or a soft pull on my personal
  credit?"

  Many:
  • Tier 1 Vendors
  • Tier 2 Vendors
  • Online Lenders

  Use Soft Pulls — or No Pull at All.

  Some online lenders use only a soft pull for initial
  qualification. Knowing this saves you score points and lets
  you apply strategically.



Removing Unauthorized Hard Inquiries

If a hard inquiry appears on your report that you did NOT
authorize — dispute it immediately.

Use Letter 2 from Chapter 10. Unauthorized inquiries are a
violation of the FCRA and can be removed. Even one
unauthorized hard pull is worth disputing because every point
matters.
