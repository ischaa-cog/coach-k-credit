 LETTER 3 — Collection Account Dispute (Not My
 Account) Use when: A collection appears that you do
 not recognize or did not incur


INSTRUCTIONS: Replace every [bracketed] item with your actual
information. Print on plain white paper. Send via USPS Certified
Mail with Return Receipt Requested. Keep a copy for your records.
Do NOT sign with an ink pen until you are ready to mail — always
use your full legal signature..

RE: Formal Dispute — Collection Account Not Belonging to Me
Account Number: [Account Number] | Original Creditor: [Name]


Dear Sir or Madam,
I am writing to formally dispute the above-referenced collection account
appearing on my credit report. This account does not belong to me. I
have no knowledge of this debt, no recollection of entering into any
agreement with [Original Creditor], and I did not authorize anyone to
incur this debt on my behalf.


Under the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA) and the Fair Credit
Reporting Act (FCRA), I am formally requesting the following:
(1) Complete verification of this debt — the original signed contract or
application bearing my signature
(2) Proof of the complete chain of ownership for this account,
documenting how your agency came to own or service this debt
(3) The full name, address, and account number of the original creditor
(4) The original date this account was opened and the date of first
delinquency
