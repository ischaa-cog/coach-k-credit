CHAPTER 2
                      FICO Deep Dive — Every
                      Factor, Every Point

      This chapter breaks down how your FICO score is actually
      built and why it matters more than other scoring models like
      VantageScore when it comes to real financial decisions such
      as mortgages, auto loans, and business funding. While
      platforms   like   Credit  Karma       often   show    higher
      VantageScores, lenders typically rely on your true FICO score,
      which can be significantly lower. Tools like IdentityIQ can
      provide access to your real FICO scores from all three
      bureaus.



       FICO is based on five core factors, and understanding them
       gives you control over nearly every part of your credit profile.



      1. Payment History (35%)

      This is the most important factor. Your score is heavily
      impacted by whether you pay on time. Even a single 30-day
      late payment can drop your score by 60–110 points. Negative
      items like collections and charge-offs are even more
      damaging, but their impact decreases over time with
      consistent positive behavior.

      The key insight is that recent payment behavior matters more
      than old mistakes. Building a consistent on-time payment
      pattern is the fastest way to recovery.

      Key actions include setting autopay, sending goodwill letters
      for late payments, and understanding that older negative
      items lose power over time—especially after two years of
      clean history.
