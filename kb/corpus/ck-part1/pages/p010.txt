                   The Three Bureaus: Why
CHAPTER 3
                   Your Scores Are Different at
                   Each One

       TransUnion, Equifax, and Experian are private companies.
       They are competitors. They do not share data with each
       other. A lender who only reports to one bureau will NOT
       appear on the other two. A dispute resolved at Equifax does
       NOT automatically fix Experian. You have to manage all
       three independently — and strategically.


       1. Transunion

       The most consumer-accessible of the three bureaus.
       TransUnion is known for being responsive to their online
       dispute portal — which is actually a strategic advantage
       you can use. Their online system sends automated
       verification requests to creditors, and many creditors
       (especially debt collectors) don't respond within the 30-day
       window. No response = deletion.

            Online dispute portal: transunion.com/credit-disputes
            Phone: 1-800-916-8800
            Mail: P.O. Box 2000, Chester, PA 19016
            Best strategy: Use the online portal aggressively for
            collections and charge-offs


       2.Equifax

       Equifax is the largest bureau in terms of data and is the
       most commonly referenced bureau for mortgage
       underwriting decisions. They were breached in 2017 — 147
       million Americans were affected. Equifax responds well to
       certified mail disputes and is very responsive to escalation
       through their executive offices.
