THE 2-PAYMENT STRATEGY: The Credit Card Hack
That Changes Your Score

This is one of the most powerful and least-known credit card
strategies. Most people make one payment per month. That
avoids late fees—but it does NOT optimize your credit score.

How Credit Card Reporting Actually Works

Your credit card balance is reported to credit bureaus once per
month on your:

👉 Statement Closing Date
NOT your due date.
Whatever balance exists on that date becomes your reported
utilization.


Why This Matters

Even if you pay your balance in full:
    If your balance is high before the statement closes
    That high balance gets reported
    Your score drops due to high utilization
