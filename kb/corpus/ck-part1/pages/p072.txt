Purchased Trade Lines — Know the Difference

You may come across services that offer to 'add trade lines' to
your credit report for a fee. These are purchased or
'piggybacked' trade lines — someone adds you as an
authorized user on their aged, positive account, temporarily
boosting your profile. While not illegal, lenders are aware of this
practice and some scoring models have been updated to
reduce the impact of purchased authorized-user trade lines.
The trade lines you BUILD through real payment activity are
always more valuable and more permanent than any
purchased trade line. Use earned trade lines. Build real
relationships with real vendors.
