 LETTER 5 — Late Payment Goodwill (Sent to Creditor
 CEO) Use when: You have a legitimate late payment
 and want it removed


INSTRUCTIONS: Replace every [bracketed] item with your actual
information. Print on plain white paper. Send via USPS Certified
Mail with Return Receipt Requested. Keep a copy for your records.
Do NOT sign with an ink pen until you are ready to mail — always
use your full legal signature.


TEMPLATE
[Your Full Legal Name]
[Your Current Mailing Address]
[City, State, ZIP Code]
[Date]


[CEO Full Name]
[Creditor Name] — Office of the Chief Executive Officer
[Creditor Address]


RE: Goodwill Request — Late Payment Adjustment
Account Number: [Account Number] | Late Payment: [Month/Year]


Dear [CEO Name],


My name is [Your Full Name], and I am a customer of [Creditor Name]
since [Account Open Date]. I am writing directly to you with the hope that
you will consider my situation and grant me a goodwill adjustment to my
credit history. In [Month/Year], I incurred a [30 / 60 / 90]-day late
payment on my account. This occurred during an extremely difficult
period in my life — [brief, honest explanation: e.g., 'I experienced an
unexpected job loss and was not able to make my payments while
navigating the transition' / 'A medical emergency in my family required
my full financial attention during this time' / 'I relocated across the
country and the payment was missed during the transition'].


I take full responsibility. I should have contacted [Creditor Name] at that
time to make arrangements, and I did not. That was a mistake, and I own
it completely.
