Charge Cards — The One Most People Don't Know About

A charge card looks and swipes exactly like a credit
card — but it is fundamentally different.

With a charge card:

    You MUST pay the full balance every month
    No option to carry a balance
    No APR on purchases
    Usually no preset spending limit
    Spending adjusts based on usage and income

The most well-known charge cards:

    American Express Green
    American Express Gold
    American Express Platinum
    American Express Centurion (Black Card)

These are CHARGE cards — not credit cards.

Because charge cards do not report a traditional credit
limit, they typically do NOT affect utilization the same way
— which can actually HELP your score.



Coach K Insight
American Express charge cards (Green, Gold, Platinum) can
be powerful credit-building tools for people who can pay in
full each month.

Because they don't report traditional limits:
✔ They don't inflate utilization
✔ They strengthen payment history
✔ They improve profile strength
⚠ But miss the full payment and consequences are severe.
Charge cards do not allow partial payments.

Know the product before you apply.
