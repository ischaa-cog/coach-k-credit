  Step 3: Freeze Your Credit at All Three Bureaus

  TransUnion, Equifax, Experian, ChexSystems, Innovis

  Step 4: File a Police Report

  Visit your local police department and file a report.
          Get a report number—many creditors require it.

  Step 5: Dispute All Fraudulent Accounts

  Use your Identity Theft Dispute letter (Chapter 10).
  Include:
      FTC Identity Theft Report
      Police Report
  Under FCRA Section 605B, bureaus must block fraudulent

  Step 6: Contact Each Creditor Directly

  Call the fraud department for every affected account.
  Request:
     Account closure
     Written confirmation
     Reporting to all bureaus as fraudulent
  Always get a case number.
