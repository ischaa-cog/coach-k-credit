The FCRA — Your Credit Bible

The Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) is the federal law that
governs everything in this book — your right to dispute, your
right to a free report, the 7-year reporting limit, the 30-day
investigation requirement, and your right to sue.

You don't need to memorize it — but you should know it
exists.
