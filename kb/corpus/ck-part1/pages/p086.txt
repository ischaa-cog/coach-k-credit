CoreLogic

Consumer Portal: corelogic.com/consumer-portal
CoreLogic collects data related to:
   Property ownership
   Mortgage activity
   Rental history

It powers tenant screening systems used by landlords.
If you’ve had:
   Evictions
   Broken leases
   Rental debt

…it is likely in your CoreLogic file—even if it does not appear on
your standard credit report.

What To Do?
  Request your CoreLogic consumer file
   Review for: Evictions, Lease violations, Rental-related debt
   Dispute inaccuracies directly

⚠️ Important:
Landlords are legally required to tell you which bureau they
used if you are denied.
