Dear Dispute Department,
I am writing to formally request the immediate removal of an
unauthorized hard
inquiry from my credit report. I did not authorize [Company Name] to
access my credit report, and I have no record of submitting any
application, request, or agreement with this company on or around
[Inquiry Date].


Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, 15 U.S.C. Section 1681b, a creditor may
only access a consumer's credit report when there is a 'permissible
purpose' — defined as an application initiated by the consumer, a
legitimate business transaction, or written authorization. None of those
conditions existed in this case.
