Your Credit Report Review Checklist
— Run This Every Time


        Accounts you don't recognize (could be fraud,
        identity theft, or a mixed file)

        Incorrect payment status (showing late when you
        paid on time)

        Duplicate accounts — the same debt listed twice
        Incorrect balances or credit limits

        Paid collections still showing a balance owed

        Discharged bankruptcy accounts still showing as
        open or owed

        Closed accounts showing as open

        Negative items older than 7 years (10 for Chapter
        7 bankruptcy)

        Incorrect employer or address information

        Hard inquiries you did not authorize

        Any judgment or tax lien (these should have been
        removed in 2018)
