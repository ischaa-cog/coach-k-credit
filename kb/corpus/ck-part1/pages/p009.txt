5. New Credit / Inquiries (10%)

TEach hard inquiry (credit application) can temporarily lower
your score by 5–10 points. Too many applications in a short
period can make you look financially unstable.
However, FICO allows rate shopping windows where multiple
inquiries for mortgages or auto loans are grouped together if
done within a short timeframe.
Smart strategies include using pre-qualification (soft
inquiries), spacing applications at least 6 months apart when
possible, and understanding that hard inquiries fall off after 2
years.
