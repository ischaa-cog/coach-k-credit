Fintech Banks & Digital Financial Institutions

Fintech (financial technology) companies have revolutionized
banking. Many now hold full banking charters or partner with FDIC-
insured institutions to offer banking products with less bureaucracy,
faster approvals, and innovative features. For people building or
rebuilding credit, fintech can be a powerful tool.
