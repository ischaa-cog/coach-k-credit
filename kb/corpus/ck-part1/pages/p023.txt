Bureau-Specific Power Moves
Experian: Use Boost First — It's Free
Experian Boost is a same-day score increase for many people.
Connect your bank account at experian.com/boost, verify your
on-time utility and streaming payments, and your Experian
score updates immediately. This only affects Experian — but
it's zero cost and takes about 5 minutes.


Equifax: Mail Disputes and Escalate to the CEO
Equifax is responsive to certified mail and to their corporate
escalation process. For serious disputes — especially on
accounts over $1,000 or multiple derogatory items — send
disputes certified mail AND send a goodwill letter to the CEO
office (Chapter 9).


All Three: Dispute the Same Item at All Three
If an error appears on one bureau, it almost certainly appears
on all three. Dispute the same item at all three bureaus
simultaneously. Do not wait for one to resolve before
addressing the others.




    Rapid Rescore:

    If you're applying for a mortgage or major
    business loan and need a score boost within
    days, ask your lender about a Rapid Rescore.
    After you make a payoff or resolve a dispute, a
    Rapid Rescore pushes the update to bureaus
    within 3–5 business days. Only lenders can order
    this — but knowing to ask for it can save your
    deal.
