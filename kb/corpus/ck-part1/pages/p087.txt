Teletrack

Consumer Report Portal: teletrack.com

Teletrack is a specialty bureau owned by TransUnion, focused on
alternative financial data.
It tracks:
     Payday loans
     Subprime installment loans
    Rent-to-own agreements
    Buy-here-pay-here auto loans
If you’ve used any of these, you likely have a Teletrack file.

What to do?
  Request your free consumer report
  Review for: Incorrect loan history & Wrong account statuses
   Dispute errors under FCRA rights
Security Freeze: Call 1-877-309-5490
Teletrack is also known as DP Bureau—same system, same rights.


Innovis

Consumer Report Portal: innovis.com

Innovis is often called the fourth credit bureau. It operates
similarly to the Big Three — collecting credit account history,
public records, and payment data — and is used by some
lenders and insurance companies. Because it is less scrutinized
than the Big Three, errors on Innovis reports often go
uncorrected for years. Getting your Innovis report and cleaning
it up is a step most credit repair guides skip entirely.

What To Do?
  Request your free annual report
  Call: 1-800-540-2505 (optional request method)
  Dispute errors online or via certified mail
  Place a free security freeze
