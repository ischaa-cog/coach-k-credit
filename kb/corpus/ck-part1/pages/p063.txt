      One of the most underused resources in the credit education
      space is the ACC — the American Consumer Credit
      organization. Most people have never heard of it. That ends
      today.

      What Is the ACC?

      The American Consumer Credit (ACC) is a non-profit
      consumer education organization that provides FREE access
      to credit education resources, credit counseling guidance,
      and financial literacy tools for everyday consumers.

      The ACC exists to level the playing field — giving everyday
      people the same financial knowledge that wealthier
      individuals pay thousands of dollars to access.

      The ACC platform includes:
      • Credit score education
      • Debt management resources
      • Consumer rights information
      • Financial planning tools

      It is an invaluable free resource that every person in this
      community should be using.
