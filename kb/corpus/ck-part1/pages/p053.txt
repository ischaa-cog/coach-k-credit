                   Credit Cards, Charge Cards
CHAPTER 12
                   &   Revolving    Accounts:
                   Know the difference

      Most people use the words credit card and charge card like
      they mean the same thing. They do not — and the difference
      matters a lot for your credit score, cash flow, and funding
      strategy.

      Let me break all three down so you never confuse them
      again.

      Credit Cards (Revolving Credit)

      A credit card is a revolving credit account. That means you
      have a set credit limit, you can charge up to that limit, carry a
      balance from month to month, and make minimum
      payments.

      The key word is REVOLVING — the credit replenishes as you
      pay it down. This is the most common type of credit product
      in America.
