Real Example
   Credit Limit: $5,000
   Spending: $1,500
   Closing Date: 15th
   Due Date: 8th

 ❌ Old Method (1 Payment)
   Pay $1,500 on due date
   Statement reports $1,500
   Utilization = 30%
   Score Impact: Negative


✅ New Method (2 Payments)
   Pay $1,400 before closing date
   Reported balance = $100 (2% utilization)
   Pay remaining $100 on due date
  Score Impact: Excellent
Same spending. Different timing. Massive results.


How to Find Your Closing Date

   Log into your credit card account and look for:
   Statement date
   Closing date
   Billing cycle end date

📌 This is usually 20–25 days before your due date
Set a reminder 5 days before to make Payment 1.
