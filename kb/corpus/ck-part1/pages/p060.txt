Credit unions are member-owned, nonprofit financial
cooperatives — and they are consistently more flexible than big
banks.

They typically offer:
       More flexible credit approvals
       Lower interest rates
       Credit builder loans
       Business checking with no minimum balance
       Personal loans at lower rates
       More personal underwriting

For someone rebuilding credit or building business credit from
scratch, a credit union relationship is often the BEST first step.


   Coach K Recommendation:

   Open a checking AND savings account at both a big bank
   (Chase or BofA) AND a local credit union. The big bank
   relationship gives you access to premium products down
   the road. The credit union gives you flexibility and better
   loan terms today. Having both is the move.



Community Banks & CDFIs

Community Development Financial Institutions (CDFIs) are
mission-driven lenders specifically designed to serve
underserved communities — including minority-owned
businesses, women entrepreneurs, and low-to-moderate
income borrowers. CDFIs often have grant programs, low-
interest loans, and technical assistance that large banks simply
don't offer.
     Opportunity Finance Network (ofn.org) — Find CDFIs in
     your area
     U.S. Treasury CDFI Fund — cdfi.treas.gov — Directory and
     program information

     Many CDFIs participate in SBA lending AND have their own
     proprietary loan programs

     Some CDFIs offer micro-grants AND micro-loans to
     businesses that can't qualify elsewhere
