Experian RentBureau (Website: experian.com/rentbureau)
Experian’s internal rent reporting system.

   Works through participating property management
   companies
   If your landlord is enrolled, your rent may already be reported


Coach K Note:
Always check with your landlord first—this could already be active
at no cost to you.

LevelCredit (formerly RentTrack) Website: levelcredit.com
Reports rent AND utility payments to TransUnion and Equifax. Also
offers a credit card product tied to your payment history. Can
report up to 24 months of past rental payments.

Coach K Note:
Strong option for adding both rent and utility history at the same
time.


Rental Kharma (Website: rentalkharma.com)
Reports rent to TransUnion and Equifax. Requires verification of
your lease and payment history. Good for people with
consistent long term rental history to add to their file.

Coach K Note:
Best suited for renters with 12+ months of consistent payment
history.
