The Rate-Shopping Exception — FICO's Built-In
Protection

FICO understands that smart consumers shop around for the
best rates. So they built in a Rate-Shopping Window. Multiple
mortgage, auto loan, or student loan inquiries within: 14–45
days (depending on FICO version) Count as ONE single inquiry.

This means you can apply with:
   5 mortgage lenders, 4 auto lenders, and multiple student
loan lenders…and your score only sees ONE inquiry.

Pro Tip:
Always rate shop within a 2-week window to be safe.

Soft Inquiries (Soft Pulls) — These NEVER Affect Your Score

Soft inquiries happen all the time without your knowledge —
and none of them affect your score. While they appear on
your report, they are visible only to YOU, not to lenders
reviewing your file.
