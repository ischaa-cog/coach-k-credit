 Additional Strategies to Maximize This System

   Set autopay (minimum payment) as a safety net
   Make weekly payments if you use your card heavily
   Request your closing date from your issuer (some will
   adjust it)
   Prioritize this strategy on high-limit cards first
   Remember: utilization is calculated
   Per card
   Across all cards
