What Are Net-30, Net-60, and Net-90 Vendor
Accounts?

Net terms define when payment is due:
• Net-30 = Payment due in 30 days
• Net-60 = Payment due in 60 days
• Net-90 = Payment due in 90 days

These are not credit cards — they are trade accounts where
you order goods or services, receive an invoice, and pay that
invoice within the agreed window. When you pay on time (or
early), the vendor reports that payment behavior to D&B; and
other business bureaus — and that builds your PAYDEX score.
