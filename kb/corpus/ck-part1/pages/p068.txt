Coach K's Freeze Recommendation: If you are not actively applying
for credit right now, freeze your credit at all three major bureaus AND
at ChexSystems. It takes 15 minutes total. It is free. It blocks identity
thieves from opening accounts in your name completely. When you
are ready to apply for something, lift the freeze online in 60 seconds,
apply, and re-freeze. This is the move.
