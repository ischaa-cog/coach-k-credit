Secondary Credit Bureaus — Hidden Files
Beyond the Big Three, there are secondary bureaus collecting
data on you.

You have FCRA rights with ALL of them.


Secondary Bureau List

       ChexSystems - Tracks banking history

       and closures
       Access: earlywarning.com

       Access: consumer.risk.lexisnexis.com

       Access: clarityservices.com
