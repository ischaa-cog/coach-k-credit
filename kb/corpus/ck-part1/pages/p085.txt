                    Secondary Bureaus Deep
CHAPTER 21
                    Dive: LexisNexis, CoreLogic,
                    Teletrack & Innovis
      The three major credit bureaus get all the attention—but
      secondary bureaus are quietly collecting data that affects:

          Insurance rates
          Apartment approvals
          Bank account access
          Lending decisions

      Most consumers never realize this.

      You have the same rights under the FCRA (Fair Credit
      Reporting Act) with these agencies as you do with the Big
      Three:
         You can pull your reports
         You can dispute errors
         You can place security freezes
      Most people have never done any of that. That changes now.


      LexisNexis Risk Solutions

      Consumer Report Portal:
      lexisnexis.com/en-us/personal/consumer-disclosure-center


      LexisNexis is one of the largest data brokers in the world. It
      collects:
          Public records
          Address history
          Property ownership records
          Court filings
          Professional licenses
          Insurance claims data


      Insurance companies rely heavily on LexisNexis to determine
      your risk profile, which directly impacts your premiums.
