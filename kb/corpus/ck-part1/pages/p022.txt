       Here's one of the most talked-about strategies in my
       community — and I'm going to walk you through
       exactly why it works. TransUnion's online dispute
       portal is your fastest dispute route, and the reason is
       rooted in how their verification system handles third-
       party debt collectors.


        The TransUnion Online Dispute Method

       Step 1: Go to TransUnion.com and create your free account
       Step 2: Access your full credit report and identify ALL
       negative items
       Step 3:       Dispute      every     unverifiable      negative       item    —
       collections,      charge-offs,       late    payments,         unrecognized
       accounts
       Step 4: Do NOT dispute legitimate, verified accounts you
       need (active mortgage, car loan you're paying)
       Step 5: When TransUnion receives your dispute, they send an
       automated verification request to the creditor
       Step 6: The creditor has 30 days to respond with PROOF that
       the account is accurate
       Step 7: Many debt collectors — especially on older accounts
       — do not respond in time
       Step 8: No response = automatic deletion under FCRA law
       Step 9: Screenshot your report before and after — document
       every win
       Step 10: If items survive: escalate to certified mail and then
       direct creditor disputes
