     away. Most people take loans without truly understanding what
     they cost. This chapter breaks everything down clearly, so you
     never sign a loan agreement again without knowing exactly
     what you agreed to.

     What is a term loan?
     A term loan is a lump sum of money you borrow and repay over
     a fixed period of time with a predetermined repayment schedule.
     Each payment includes:

        Principal — the amount you borrowed
        Interest — the cost of borrowing
     Term loans are commonly used for: Business expansion,
     Equipment purchases, Real estate, Vehicle financing, and
     Personal expenses
