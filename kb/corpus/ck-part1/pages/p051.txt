CHAPTER 11          Your    90-Day   Credit
                    Repair Game Plan
     Exact Steps, In Order to give you the exact same framework I walk
     my clients through. This is not motivational content. These are
     operational steps. Follow them in order and you will see real,
     measurable change within 90 days.


      WEEK 1: FOUNDATION — Know What You're Working With

      ✔ Pull all three bureau reports at AnnualCreditReport.com
      ✔ Sign up for IdentityIQ for daily monitoring
      ✔ Sign up for Experian Boost — immediate free points on Experian
      ✔ Sign up for Ecredable Personal to add utility payment history
      ✔ Build your dispute spreadsheet:
           Creditor
           Account Number
           Bureau(s)
           Reason
           Age
           Balance
           Strategy
      ✔   Categorize every negative:
           (A) Clear errors
           (B) Expired items (7+ years)
           (C) Legitimate but challengeable
      ✔   Identify unauthorized hard inquiries — use Letter 2 for each one


           WEEKS 2–4: DISPUTE BLITZ — Hit All Three Bureaus

      ✔ Use Dispute Beast to generate letters for Category A errors
      ✔ Send online disputes to TransUnion for unverifiable collections and
      charge-offs
      ✔ Send certified mail disputes to Equifax (Track every certified number)
      ✔ Send certified mail disputes to Experian
      ✔ Send 609 Letters (Letter 8) for accounts over 4 years old
      ✔ Send Direct Creditor Disputes (Letter 9) for payment history errors
      ✔ Pay down credit cards above 30% utilization
      ✔ Push balances below 10% for maximum impact
      ✔ Open Credit Strong account to add installment history
