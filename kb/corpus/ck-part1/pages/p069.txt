     When I talk to entrepreneurs about building business credit,

     "How do I get accounts that actually report and build my
     score?"
     The answer is trade lines and vendor accounts — specifically
     Net-30/60/90 vendors. This chapter breaks down exactly how
     they work and which ones to target first.

      What Is a Trade Line?

      A trade line is any account that appears on a credit report.

      Examples: Credit cards, Loans, Vendor accounts, Lines of credit
      For business credit, trade lines are the Dun & Bradstreet (D&B)
      requires at least THREE trade lines reporting before generating
      a PAYDEX score.
      No trade lines reporting = No PAYDEX score = No business
      credit profile
      Your First Goal:
      Get 3 trade lines reporting. Everything else comes after.
