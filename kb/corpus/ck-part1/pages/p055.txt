Revolving Accounts — The Broader Category

Revolving credit is the CATEGORY that includes credit cards,
lines of credit, and home equity lines of credit (HELOCs).
What makes them revolving is the cycle: you borrow, you
repay, the credit is available again. This is different from
installment accounts (car loans, mortgages, student loans)
which have a fixed repayment schedule with a defined end
date.

The ideal credit profile for maximum FICO scoring has
BOTH revolving accounts (credit cards/lines of credit) AND
installment accounts (a car loan, mortgage, or credit
builder loan).

Having only one type limits your score potential regardless
of your payment history. Credit mix accounts for 10% of
your FICO score — and that 10% is easy to earn with the
right strategy.
