                    Identity Theft: How to
CHAPTER 18
                    Detect It, Report It, and
                    Recover From It

      Identity theft is one of the fastest-growing financial crimes in
      America. Over 1.4 million identity theft reports are filed with the
      FTC every year.


      In the financial world, it most often appears as:
          Fraudulent accounts opened in your name
         Unauthorized credit inquiries
         Charges on existing accounts you didn’t make
         Tax returns filed using your Social Security number before
         you file yours

      If you don’t catch it quickly, it can devastate a credit profile
      you’ve spent years building.


      This chapter gives you everything you need to detect it early,
      stop it fast, and fully recover your credit when it happens.



      Warning Signs That Your Identity May Have Been Stolen

      • Accounts on your credit report you don’t recognize
      • Hard inquiries you didn’t authorize
      • Bills or collection calls for debts you never incurred
      • Being denied credit unexpectedly
      • Medical bills for services you never received
      • Tax return rejected as one was already filed using your SSN
      • Missing mail (mail redirection is a common tactic)
      • Unknown charges on bank or credit card statements
      • Alerts about new accounts you didn’t open
      • Debt collectors contacting you about unknown accounts

      If you notice even one of these — take action immediately.
