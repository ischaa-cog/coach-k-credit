     Your rent is likely the largest monthly payment you make. For
     most of history, that payment built nothing on your credit
     report. Landlords didn’t report to credit bureaus, meaning you
     could pay $1,500 a month for years and gain zero credit
     benefit.


     That has changed. Today, there are tools that allow your rent
     payments to be reported to one, two, or all three major credit
     bureaus—turning an expense you already have into a credit-
     building asset.


     Rent Reporting Services — Turn Your Rent Into
     Credit History


     Rent Reporters (Website: rentreporters.com)
     Reports rent payment history to TransUnion and Equifax. Can
     report up to 24 months of past rent history, not just going
     forward. This is powerful — you can get credit for rent you
     already paid. Average score increase reported is 35-40 points.
     Monthly subscription service.

     Coach K Note:
     One    of    the    most     established        rent    reporting      services.
     Backdating up to 2 years is a major advantage.


     Boom (Website: boom.credit)
     Boom is a comprehensive financial profile builder that reports
     rent, utilities, and subscriptions to all three major credit
     bureaus — TransUnion, Equifax, AND Experian. Boom also offers
     rent-secured credit options and financial coaching tools.
