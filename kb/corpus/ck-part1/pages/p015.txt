                   The Truth Nobody Tells You:
CHAPTER 6          Most Grantors Don't Even
                   Pull Credit
      I need to pause here because this is one of those things that
      completely changed how I teach business credit — and once
      you understand it, you stop moving the same way.

      Here’s the truth: most business credit grantors in Tier 1 and
      Tier 2 (vendors and suppliers) are not even looking at your
      personal credit. They are not focused on your FICO score, your
      past mistakes, bankruptcies, or charge-offs.

      What they care about is much simpler — and honestly, much
      more controllable:

      Does your business look real and properly set up?



      What Grantors Are Actually Looking At

      Instead of your personal credit report, they are checking
      whether your business passes basic legitimacy signals:

         Is your business properly registered with the state?
         Do you have an EIN that matches your business identity?
         Is your business phone number listed in 411 or public
         directories?
         Does your address match across all registrations?
         Do you have a real website and professional business
         email?
         How long has your business been open? (even 30–90
         days matters)

      What they are not usually focused on at this stage is your
      personal credit score.

      That’s the part most people get wrong.
