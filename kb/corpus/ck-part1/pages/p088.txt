Your Complete Secondary Bureau Action Plan

Most people only focus on the Big Three. That is a mistake.

These steps take 2–3 hours total and can unlock:


   Lower insurance premiums
   Better rental approvals
   Improved banking access

Action Steps
   Pull reports from:
       LexisNexis
       CoreLogic
       Teletrack
        Innovis
        ChexSystems
        Early Warning Services (EWS)
   Review each report for:
      Errors
      Outdated information
      Inaccurate negative items
   Dispute all inaccuracies using FCRA rights
   Place security freezes at all bureaus not in use
   Set a reminder to review reports annually
