



    The 6 Secret Steps to Building
    Business Credit
    Introduction
    In this book we show you the 6 Steps to Building Business Credit.
    Steps 1 and 2 are foundational. It is critical to complete them so your business
    is ready to build strong business credit scores. Without completing Steps 1 and
    2, it is impossible to build business credit scores separate from your personal
    credit scores.
    Steps 3, 4, 5 & 6 are where you actually build business credit. Step 3 is about
    setting up with all the business credit agencies: Equifax, Experian and Dun
    & Bradstreet. Step 4 is for getting five vendor lines of credit reporting to the
    agencies. Step 5 is for getting three business credit cards reporting to the
    agencies. Step 6 is all about getting your first business bank loan reporting to
    the business credit agencies.
    Below is a summary of each step and what you must do to successfully build
    business credit.

    Step 1 – Set The Foundation For Building
    Business Credit
    Step 1 –This foundational step is all about
    establishing credibility for your company. Think
    about it from a lender’s perspective. They are in
    business to lend to companies they consider to
    be safe risks. They perform several underwriting
    checks to see if you are safe enough for them to
    consider extending credit.
    Part of establishing yourself as a safe risk starts by being in compliance.
    Being in compliance helps you establish credibility for your company. This is
    foundational to your success!
