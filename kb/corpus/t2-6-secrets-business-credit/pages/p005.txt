Step 4 – The Vendor Credit Tier
(Net 30 Day Accounts)
Step 4 is all about vendor credit.
A vendor line of credit is when a company (vendor) extends a line of credit to
your business on Net 30, 60 or 90 day terms. This means you can buy their
products or services up to a certain dollar amount. And you have 30, 60 or 90
days to pay the bill in full. So if you buy $300 worth of goods today, then the
$300 is due within the next 30 days.
