    Step 3 – Get Set Up With All Three Business
    Credit Reporting Agencies
    Step 3 is all about the business credit reporting agencies.
    In Step 3, we show you how to open and kick off your business credit files. This
    is with all three business credit reporting agencies. They are: Dun & Bradstreet,
    Experian, and Equifax.
    Most vendors use Dun & Bradstreet to extend
    lines of credit. Landlords use them to approve
    office leases as well. Many credit card companies
    and nontraditional business lenders use Experian.
    Equifax has the Small Business Financial Exchange.
    It is most important for cash lenders such as banks.
    To concentrate on one and not the others creates
    lopsided credibility. You need to build all three.
    The how-to instructions in this Step clarify how to get started the right way
    with each Business Credit Reporting Agency. These methods have been tested
